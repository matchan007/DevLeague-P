# Email Review System Audit — 2026-08-26

Scope: Leo's email review pipeline — the "Sarah" sweep/brief agent, the legacy
skimmer/thinker routines, the Gmail label system, and the Notion HQ
(Queue + Register) surfaces. All findings verified against live Gmail state,
the Routines list, session history, and the Notion databases on 2026-08-26
(UTC), ~02:35–02:50.

## Verdict

**The Gmail half is healthy. The Notion half has never run. The legacy
routines are dead and should be retired.**

Sarah is sweeping on schedule and triaging mail well — the inbox sat at one
unread message at audit time, and that message had already been labeled. But
the HQ dashboard in Notion, which is designed to be the place where things
"need you," is empty and stale: the Queue has zero rows ever, the Register
hasn't changed since it was seeded on Aug 17, and the HQ heartbeat still
reads "Last sweep: not yet running." Meanwhile real deadlines that arrived by
email (Camp Erdman due 8/28, Dropbox data-deletion Sep 8, a payment failure)
exist only as Gmail labels, and seven unsent drafts are waiting with no
surface that shows them.

## Findings (ranked)

### 1. The Notion pipeline has never run 🔴
The Queue database contains **zero rows** — the Approve/Reject decision loop
that the HQ page is built around has never carried a single item. The
Register still holds only the 8 obligations seeded at setup (Aug 17), all
still "Open," while obligation-bearing mail has since arrived and exists
only as Gmail labels: Camp Erdman trip **due 8/28**, Dropbox
over-quota **deletion Sep 8**, a Hulu payment failure, Magnus Health
incomplete-requirements notices for both kids. The HQ heartbeat callout
still says "Mon 17 Aug · Last sweep: not yet running." Either wire the
sweep to post into Queue/Register and stamp the heartbeat, or remove the
Notion layer — today it silently under-reports.

### 2. "Needs you" items have no surface 🔴
Because the Queue is empty, the things waiting on Leo accumulate invisibly:
7 unsent drafts (two Japanese-business drafts pending since **Aug 19**;
oldest draft Jul 12), 7 unread under `Sarah/Unsubscribe`, and a
potential-client inquiry ("Real Estate Consulting," Aug 21) labeled
Needs-Reply but archived with **no draft** — a possible missed lead (or a
scam solicitation; either way it should have been surfaced for a decision,
not shelved).

### 3. Legacy routines are dead but not retired 🟠
The skimmer and thinker Routines still exist with stale schedules. They
never recovered after the Aug 19 limit rejection (the limit reset Aug 20;
they still didn't fire — stuck). If they ever revive they will
double-process the inbox with **conflicting rules** (they trash and archive
aggressively, use different labels, and the skimmer pokes the thinker).
Retire them deliberately: disable or delete both, and sweep the leftover
state (21 threads still under `NeedsThinker`, 3 under `Unsub?`).

### 4. Single point of failure: the laptop 🟠
Sarah runs through Remote Control from Leo's machine. Session history shows
`computer_unreachable` init failures on Aug 22–23 — sweeps silently don't
happen when the machine is asleep or offline, and nothing alerts on a
missed sweep. A cheap fix: have the morning brief check the timestamp of
the last successful sweep and flag a gap > 4 h during daytime.

### 5. Model/cost sizing 🟡
The Aug 19 outage was caused by running the *light* hourly tier on the most
expensive model until the weekly limit tripped. The current sweeps run on a
top-tier 1M-context model every 2 hours. The sweep tier is a
classification task — a smaller model would cut cost and limit-risk
substantially; keep the big model for the brief/judgment tier.

### 6. Label hygiene 🟡
Three generations of taxonomy coexist (`Agent/*`, `NeedsThinker`/`Unsub?`,
`Sarah/*` — plus unused `Priority`, `Sarah` parent, `Ben`, `Calendar`,
`Filed` variants). Collapse to the current generation once the legacy
routines are retired.

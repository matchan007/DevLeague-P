# MEMORY — Fee simple land value

Everything here is about **unencumbered fee simple land**: owned outright, forever,
free of any ground lease. This is the only interest the project actually cares about.
Leased fee and leasehold are kept separate in `02-ground-lease.md`.

---

## The working numbers

Best estimate of fee simple land value in the Waikiki Special District, as of Aug 2026:

| Location | Fee simple $/sf of land |
|---|---|
| Kalakaua Ave — small parcel or corner (under ~40,000 sf) | **$1,600 – 2,000** |
| Kalakaua Ave — oceanfront / beach frontage | **$1,500 – 1,800** |
| Kuhio Ave corridor | **$1,200 – 1,500** |
| Large block (200,000+ sf) | **$1,100 – 1,300** |
| Interior / Ala Wai — Apartment Precinct | **not measured** — no comps exist in this study |

Single working number for a mid-size fee simple parcel in the resort corridor:
**~$1,400/sf**. Underwrite Kuhio at $1,300; be sceptical above $1,600 without beach
frontage or grandfathered FAR above 5.0.

**Status of these figures: inference, not measurement.** They are derived by reading
leased-fee sales as fee-simple proxies. Replace them with RPAD data (below).

## The only true fee-simple data point in the set

**Bank of Hawaii Waikiki Center ground — 2155 Kalakaua Ave at Beach Walk**
$67,000,000 / 33,500 sf = **$2,000/sf**, recorded 27 Mar 2026.
Seller: Kapuahelani LP. Buyer: Shoei Global (Shoei USA affiliate, Japan).

Why it matters: **the lessee bought its own fee**, so the ground lease *merged and was
extinguished at closing* — the buyer walked away holding clean unencumbered fee simple
land. Every other land comp in the study conveyed a leased fee.

Why it's a ceiling, not a midpoint: a tenant buying out its own ground rent pays
marriage value that no third-party buyer would.

Historical: Kapuahelani acquired the same ground in 1995 for ~$4.0M (~$119/sf).
16.8× over 31 years = **9.5% compound annual** appreciation on raw Waikiki land.

## HOW TO GET REAL FEE SIMPLE NUMBERS — the method that matters

The City of Honolulu already performs this exact analysis on every parcel in Waikiki,
annually, and publishes it free. This is the shortest path to the project's goal.

### Why it works

Three provisions of the Revised Ordinances of Honolulu:

- **ROH §8-6.1** — all real property is taxed on **100% of its fair market value**
- Property is assessed **"in its entirety"**, which is interpreted as the
  **fee simple interest** — leases are ignored
- **ROH §8-7.1** — assessment uses the **market data (sales comparison) and cost
  approaches**, developing land values by **subtracting building values from
  comparable sale prices**

That last step *is* land extraction — the same procedure an appraiser charges for,
run annually on every parcel. And RPAD reports **Assessed Land Value** and
**Assessed Building Value** as separate line items.

### The procedure

1. Go to **https://www.qpublic.net/hi/honolulu/search.html**
   (or https://realproperty.honolulu.gov/) — free, no login
2. Search by street address or TMK
3. Record two fields: **Land Area** (sq ft) and **Assessed Land Value**
4. **Divide** → fee simple dollars per square foot of land
5. Repeat for 15–20 parcels: the target, its immediate neighbours, and a spread across
   Kalakaua, Kuhio, and the Ala Wai side
6. Sort by $/sf → a fee-simple land value map of Waikiki, from the city's own numbers

Also capture while you're in the record: **TMK**, **zoning**, **last sale price and
date**, and **assessed building value**.

Data refreshes Monday mornings.

### Two limitations

- **Lag.** Values are set as of **1 October of the prior year**, so they trail a moving
  market.
- **Mass appraisal.** Reliable across 20 parcels; occasionally wrong on a single odd one.

**Free calibration for both:** any parcel that sold recently shows its **sale price and
its assessed value on the same page**. Compare the two across several recent sales and
you know which direction and how far assessments are running against the market.

### If more depth is needed

- **Hawaii Bureau of Conveyances** — recorded deeds, grantor/grantee, and the
  **conveyance tax stamp**, which is computed on actual consideration and therefore
  reveals undisclosed prices (e.g. the Queen Kapiolani sale)
- **A commercial brokerage with CoStar / Honolulu Board of Realtors access** — the only
  way to close the Apartment Precinct gap; that data is licensed
- **A Hawaii state-certified general appraiser** with Waikiki hospitality experience —
  required before committing capital. This study is research, not an appraisal.

## Zoning drives fee simple value

Every parcel carries an underlying LUO zoning district plus a Waikiki Special District
precinct laid over it. The precinct usually binds, and it decides whether transient
vacation (hotel) use — the highest-value use in Waikiki — is permitted as of right.

| Precinct | Hotel use | Effect on land value |
|---|---|---|
| Resort Mixed Use | as of right | premium; all four primary comps sit here |
| Resort | as of right | premium |
| Apartment | **not** as of right | materially lower; no comps measured |
| Apartment Mixed Use | **not** as of right | between the two; unmeasured |
| Public | n/a | not a market category |

Height limits step down toward Diamond Head: **350 ft** at the Ala Moana end,
**220 ft** at the Diamond Head end.

PD-R (Planned Development – Resort) density: base **4.0 FAR**; where existing FAR
already exceeds 3.33, up to a 20% increase capped at **5.0 FAR**; above 5.0, the
existing FAR is the cap.

**Land is really a price for buildable floor area.** At 4.0 FAR, $1,315/sf of land is
$329 per buildable sf; at 5.0 FAR it is $263. **Grandfathered FAR above 5.0 cannot be
recreated and should be priced separately** — confirm existing FAR before valuing land.

## What a land number does NOT include

Land value is not acquisition cost. Excluded: any building on the site; title, escrow,
Hawaii conveyance tax, brokerage and legal; entitlement and permitting plus the carry;
demolition and site work; tenant buyouts or relocation; and — if a ground lease exists —
the leasehold interest, which can exceed the land itself several times over.

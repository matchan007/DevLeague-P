## What the data says

1. **Parcel size drives price more than date.** No usable time trend across the four
   primary comps — a 2019 sale ($1,791) sits above a 2025 sale ($1,137). Size and
   frontage explain the spread. Under ~35,000 sf, the small-lot comps govern.
2. **Frontage premium ≈ 27%.** ʻAlohilani (oceanfront Kalakaua, 2.5 ac) at $1,791/sf vs
   Hilton Waikiki Beach (2 blocks mauka on Kuhio, 1.27 ac — *smaller*, same year, same
   seller) at $1,410/sf. About as clean a location isolation as this market offers.
3. **The Apartment Precinct is unmeasured.** Every primary comp is on Kalakaua or Kuhio.
   No verified interior/Ala Wai land comp exists. Do not extrapolate $1,315/sf there.

## Verification checklist

1. Pull TMK and true land area for every comp (RPAD) — can move a $/sf figure 10–15%
2. Pull each recorded deed and read the **conveyance tax stamp** (Bureau of Conveyances)
3. Read the **ground lease** on every leased-fee comp — rent, term, resets, reversion
4. Confirm the target parcel's precinct and underlying zoning (DPP / HoLIS)
5. Confirm the target's **existing FAR** and height envelope
6. Close the Apartment Precinct gap via a brokerage with CoStar / HBR access
7. Verify the two flagged rows (#6 Waikiki Trade Center, #7 Kuhio studio building)
8. Check encumbrances: easements, shoreline setback, WSD design review, historic, tenancies
9. Commission a Hawaii state-certified general appraisal before committing capital
